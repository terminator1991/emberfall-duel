EMBERFALL DUEL

A CARD DUEL OF RESONANCE & COUNTERS

Two duelists, one 44-card deck, 20 Life each. Every clash, both play a card: it strikes for its rank, +2 if its suit echoes your last suit (Resonance), +3 if its suit counters the foe's card — Ember burns Grove, Grove cracks Stone, Stone dams Tide, Tide douses Ember. The higher strike wounds for the difference. Wild Sigils strike a flat 6 and cannot be countered. When the deck runs dry, hunger bites.
